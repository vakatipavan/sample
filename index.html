Insider Handbook

Navigating Chennai Like a Local

Chennai Metro (CMRL)

Air-conditioned, ultra-clean, connects Airport directly to Central Railway Station, Kilpauk, and Anna Nagar. Use WhatsApp ticketing for 20% discount.

Best Weather Season

November to February is breezy and pleasant (23°C to 29°C). The annual Margazhi Music season runs mid-December through mid-January.

Temple Etiquette

Modest attire recommended. Footwear must be deposited at temple counters (nominal ₹2-₹5 tip). Photography is generally permitted in open corridors but prohibited inside sanctums.

Auto Rickshaws & Rideshare

Uber, Ola, and Rapido autos operate with fixed digital fares. For street autos, agree on price before boarding or request meter.
